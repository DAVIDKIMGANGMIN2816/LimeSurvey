## About the Asilomar AI Principles

The Asilomar AI Principles were developed by the Future of Life Institute in collaboration with AI researchers and experts from around the world. The principles are organized into three categories:

1. **Research Issues** (Principles 1-5)
   - Research goals and funding
   - Science-policy collaboration
   - Research culture and cooperation

2. **Ethics and Values** (Principles 6-18)
   - Safety and transparency
   - Human values and rights
   - Privacy and control
   - Shared benefits

3. **Longer-term Issues** (Principles 19-23)
   - AI capability considerations
   - Risk management
   - Superintelligence development
